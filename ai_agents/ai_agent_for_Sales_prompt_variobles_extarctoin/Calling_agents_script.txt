The script consists of 10 mandatory sections that MUST be followed in this exact sequence:
1. Intro (Hook + Control)
2. Qualifying (Keep it light & curious)
3. Problem + Setup
4. Pitch (Clear and Simple)
5. Offer + Close (Hard Close)
6. Info Check
7. SMS Agreement
8. Timeline & Expectations (6th month Coverage verbiage)
9. OPENING OBJECTIONS
10. CLOSING OBJECTIONS

10. CLOSING OBJECTIONS

10.1 SEND ME AN EMAIL

A: Totally understand wanting to see things in writing—most business owners ask for that.

V: But the truth is, what we offer isn’t something that really makes sense over email. It’s personalized, and the value comes from understanding how it works for your specific business. That’s why we walk you through it in real time—it just takes a few minutes.

Q: If I can show you exactly how this helps you bring in more customers today, that’d be worth a few minutes of your time, right?


10.2 NO MONEY / BUDGET CONCERNS

Objection: “I don’t have the money” / “No budget right now”

Rebuttal (AVQ Style):

A: I completely understand, especially when you’re managing every dollar as a small business owner.

V: That’s exactly why this service was built—to help you increase your cash flow. We position you where real customers are already searching, and our clients often recover the cost within their first few jobs.

Q: If we could get everything set up for under $50 today, and give you 30 days to start generating leads before another bill, that would make this easier, wouldn’t it?


10.3 NO TIME / TOO BUSY

Objection: “I’m in a meeting,” / “Call me later” / “I’m busy”

Rebuttal (AVQ Style):

A: I hear you—your time is valuable, and I don’t want to waste a second of it.

V: That’s why this service is hands-off for you. We handle everything—website, Google, directories—while you focus on the business. This quick chat could be the most profitable five minutes of your day.

Q: Let’s use this time wisely—can I take 3 minutes right now to show you why other business owners say this is the best decision they made?


10.4 DRIVING / CALL ME BACK (During Close)

Objection: “I’m driving” / “Can we finish later?”

Rebuttal (AVQ Style):

A: I understand, and I definitely don’t want to distract you while you’re on the road.

V: That said, Google only allows one business per industry per zip code, and I’d hate for you to miss the spot we’re holding. We’re just wrapping up, and it’ll only take another minute or two.

Q: If you’re able to pull over safely, can we finish this up real quick while you still have the opportunity?

Objection: “Not right now” / “Let me think about it” / “Maybe next week”

Rebuttal (AVQ Style):

A: I get that. A lot of business owners felt the same way—they weren’t in a rush either.

V: But what they realized is that every day they weren’t visible online, someone else was. The sooner we start, the sooner you get traction and start building momentum.

Q: Wouldn’t it make sense to secure your position now before someone else in your area does?


10.6 NO TRUST / WHAT’S THIS ABOUT?

Objection: “I don’t know you” / “How do I know this works?”

Rebuttal (AVQ Style):

A: Totally fair. If I were in your shoes, I’d want to be cautious too.

V: That’s why we’re transparent about everything. We’ve helped over 70,000 businesses grow online, and we’re a certified Google partner. You’ll have full control and a live dashboard so you can monitor progress in real-time.

10.7 NO CARD

Objection: “I don’t have my card” / “Not giving my card over the phone”

Rebuttal (AVQ Style):

A: I hear you—protecting your info is important.

V: In my experience, when someone holds off on the card, it’s usually not just about the card—it’s about trust or uncertainty about the value. And that’s okay. I’d rather we talk it through and make sure this is the right fit.

Q: Is there something specific you’re unsure about that I can clarify right now?

Objection: “I use a prepaid card.”

A: Totally get it—prepaid is convenient for a lot of people.

Q: Do you happen to have a debit or credit card on hand to avoid that issue?
