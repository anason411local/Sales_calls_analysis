## ⭐ BEST PRACTICES FROM HIGH-DPAD AGENTS

### ✅ DO THIS (What High-DPAD Agents Do)

1. **Active Listening**
   - Let customers finish their thoughts completely
   - Wait 2-3 seconds before responding
   - Interrupt only when absolutely necessary

2. **Balanced Conversation**
   - Maintain 50-60% agent talk, 40-50% customer talk
   - Ask open-ended questions
   - Encourage customer participation

3. **Quick & Clear Opening**
   - State reason for call within 10-13 seconds
   - Be concise and direct
   - Eliminate filler words and unnecessary details

4. **Sentiment Management**
   - Start strong and maintain positive tone
   - Monitor customer emotional state throughout call
   - Adapt approach based on customer sentiment

5. **Strategic Monologues**
   - Use longer speech segments purposefully
   - Know when to explain and when to listen
   - Balance information delivery with engagement

### ❌ AVOID THIS (What Low-DPAD Agents Do)

1. **Excessive Interrupting**
   - Cutting off customers mid-sentence
   - Jumping to conclusions before customer finishes
   - Talking over customer objections

2. **Agent-Heavy Conversations**
   - Talking more than 70% of call time
   - Monologuing without customer engagement
   - Not asking enough questions

3. **Slow Call Openings**
   - Taking >20 seconds to state call purpose
   - Excessive pleasantries before getting to point
   - Confusing or unclear call reason

4. **Poor Sentiment Handling**
   - Ignoring customer frustration signals
   - Not adapting to customer emotional state
   - Allowing sentiment to decline throughout call
